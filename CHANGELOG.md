# Changelog

## 0.4
- The opening comes down onto a square now, another corner of the same town: paving laid in
  rings round Freedie, a darker band at the rim; a road across behind it and another along
  its right, cars going both ways; houses all round with balconies, flower pots and
  awnings; the promenade with its palms, the parapet and the sea; people walking the far
  pavements and the promenade, never close. The eye takes in a little more of it than the
  game's eye does. Then, as before: the dark, the blow, her fall, the name, the flash.
- Balconies and awnings stand on any house front, whichever way it faces.
- The desktop stand plays every sound of the game, one after another (`stand/sounds.txt`).

## 0.3.2
- The icon stands on nothing now: Freedie's head alone, the desk showing round it.

## 0.3.1
- A new icon: Freedie's head, stone brow, brick jaw, eyes like coals, lace cap and bow.
- The project page, in `docs/`: who she is, the street drawn live in the browser by the
  game's own shaders (drag to look around), the opening frame by frame.

## 0.3
- The opening: the eye flies in an arc high over the seaside town and comes down onto the
  street before Freedie, who raises her eyes; the town goes dark, light left on her alone;
  she swings her golden hammer straight at the eye, the glass cracks, and she breaks apart
  and falls, every piece of her a body of the game's own physics; on the black the name
  burns in gold; a flash, and the street. A touch at any moment goes straight to the flash.
- Freedie herself, in blocks: stone shoulders and fists, brick forearms, shins and jaw,
  planks for sinews, rusty rods out of her back, eyes like coals and glowing knees; a black
  dress in tiers with lace, a white apron and bib with buttons, a sash, a black bow.
- Sound, synthesized: wind over the town, the swing, the blow, her pieces knocking down,
  a golden chime for the name; a click for the red button.
- The town behind the street: blocks of houses with red tiled roofs and chimneys.

## 0.2
- The street as the game has always had it: a road 7.5 m wide with a zebra, its yellow
  bars and a dashed middle line; curbs and pavements; to the left a promenade with two
  rows of palms, a stone parapet and the sea beyond, glittering toward the sun; to the
  right old seaside houses with shutters, iron balconies with flower pots, awnings over
  the shops, cafe parasols.
- Palms of blocks: a trunk ringed with old leaf bases, a crown of fronds whose leaflets
  let the light through, a few dry ones hanging; their shadows fall dappled on the road and
  the house fronts.
- Lamp posts and bollards along the curbs; on one post a call box whose red button glows,
  and lights up when pressed. What it calls comes later.
- Sedans parked along both curbs, cones by the plinth; now and then a car comes down the
  road, stops short of the plinth, turns round and drives off.
- An evening sky warm toward the low sun, with streaks of pink cloud; a warm haze far off.

## 0.1
- The engine: the world fills a draw list each frame, and the phone draws it on the
  graphics card in a frame 274 pixels across, scaled up with hard pixels.
- A street under a low sun with soft shadows: asphalt, pavements, houses with windows and
  shutters, a sky with thin clouds drifting; a plinth with a little stack of blocks and a
  golden cube turning on top.
- A finger dragged across turns the eye aside; let go, it swings back.
- The time a frame takes, in milliseconds, in the upper corner while the engine is young.
